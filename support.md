## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an account to use Pobyto?

No.

You can use the main planning features without creating an account.

Signing in enables account-based features such as syncing supported Pobyto data across devices.

---

### How do I sync my data across devices?

Sign in to Pobyto using the same account on your devices.

Data eligible for cloud sync will then be associated with your Pobyto account.

---

### How do I restore a Pro purchase?

Open the Pobyto Pro screen and select:

**Restore purchases**

Make sure you are using the same Apple ID or Google account that was originally used for the purchase.

If the purchase still does not appear after restoring, contact us at:

**alina.altynbayeva.00@gmail.com**

---

### What is included in Pobyto Pro?

Pobyto Pro provides access to additional planning and productivity features available on the Pro screen in the app.

Depending on the store and region, Pobyto may offer:

- An annual subscription
- A lifetime purchase

Current prices are always shown directly in the app before purchase.

---

### How do I cancel my annual subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store where you purchased them.

#### On iPhone or iPad

Open:

**Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Pobyto**

You can view or cancel your subscription there.

#### On Android

Open:

**Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Pobyto**

Samsung Galaxy Store purchases may be managed through the corresponding Samsung account and store purchase settings.

Deleting the Pobyto app does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

---

### Does the Lifetime purchase expire?

No.

The Lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase that unlocks the applicable Pobyto Pro features without a recurring subscription fee.

If you reinstall the app, use **Restore purchases** with the same store account that made the original purchase.

---

### I purchased Pro but the app still shows the free version. What should I do?

First try:

1. Check that you have an internet connection.
2. Open the Pro screen.
3. Select **Restore purchases**.
4. Make sure you are signed into the same store account used for the original purchase.
5. Restart Pobyto.

If the issue continues, contact:

**alina.altynbayeva.00@gmail.com**

---

### How do I delete my Pobyto account?

You can delete your account from the app's Settings screen.

Account deletion removes the Pobyto cloud data associated with that account, subject to the retention rules described in our Privacy Policy and those of third-party services used to operate the app.

Deleting your Pobyto account does not automatically cancel subscriptions managed by Apple, Google, or Samsung.

---

### Can I use Pobyto without uploading my documents?

Yes.

Pobyto does not require you to upload copies of your passport, residence card, or other identity documents.

You can use the app to track your preparation without uploading those documents.

---

### Does Pobyto submit my application to Czech authorities?

No.

Pobyto does not submit residence applications, communicate with Czech authorities on your behalf, or represent any government authority.

Pobyto is an independent organizational and informational tool.

---

### Is the information in Pobyto legal advice?

No.

Pobyto helps organize publicly available information and your own preparation.

Immigration and residence requirements can change. Always verify important requirements with the relevant Czech authority before submitting an application or taking an important legal step.

Official sources include:

- Information Portal for Foreigners of the Ministry of the Interior of the Czech Republic  
  https://ipc.gov.cz/en/

- Ministry of the Interior of the Czech Republic – Immigration  
  https://mv.gov.cz/mvcren/article/immigration.aspx

- Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Czech Republic – Entry & Residence  
  https://mzv.gov.cz/jnp/en/information_for_aliens/index.html
