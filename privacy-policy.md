## What Pobyto does

Pobyto is a guidance app that helps you to prepare for Czech residence permit applications and renewals. It does not submit applications or communicate with government authorities on your behalf.

---

## Data we collect and why

### Data you enter yourself

When you use Pobyto you may enter:
- Your residence permit type and expiry date
- Step statuses and personal notes on preparation steps
- Document preparation notes

This data is stored on your device. If you choose to create an account (email and password), this data is also synced to our cloud database (Firebase Firestore) so you can access it across devices. You can use Pobyto without an account. All features work with local storage only.

### Data collected automatically

- **Firebase** (Google): if you use the cloud sync feature, Firebase stores the data you choose to sync. Firebase may collect anonymised usage diagnostics. See [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **RevenueCat**: if you purchase a Pro subscription, RevenueCat processes the transaction and records your entitlement. RevenueCat does not receive your payment card details - those are handled by Google Play. See [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

---

## Data we do not collect

- We do not collect your passport number, national ID, or any identity document contents.
- We do not collect your location.
- We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
- We do not use your data to train AI models.

---

## Data storage and security

Data synced to the cloud is stored in Google Firebase (EU region where available). Data stored only on your device is not accessible to us.

---

## Your rights

You can:
- Delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from the Settings screen inside the app.
- Use the app without an account - no cloud data is stored.
- Contact us at the email above with any data question or deletion request.

---

## Children

Pobyto is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
